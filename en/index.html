The place

A carousel, a bar, and a party until morning

Striped big top, black-and-white checkerboard, filament bulbs: Carrousel is a fairground bar made for long nights. At the counter: house cocktails, spritz, champagne and a wide range of spirits by the glass or by the bottle.

FAQ

Good to know

Where is Carrousel?+

1 Rue Fernand David (passage bleu), 74100 Annemasse, France — in Annemasse, on the doorstep of Geneva.

What are the opening hours?+

Monday — Tuesday: Closed ; Wednesday — Friday: 18:00 — 02:00 ; Saturday: 06:00 — 12:00 · 18:00 — 02:00 ; Sunday: 06:00 — 12:00. Weekend afters start early in the morning.

Can I book a table?+

Yes. Email lecarrouselbar@gmail.com with the date, time and number of guests.

How much is a cocktail?+

All our signature and classic cocktails are €12. The full menu with every price is online at carrouselbar.fr/en/menu/.

Can I hire the bar for a private event?+

Yes, for birthdays, company parties and other celebrations. Email lecarrouselbar@gmail.com with your date and number of guests.

Is the menu available in other languages?+

Yes: the digital menu is available in French, English, Italian, German and Spanish, via the QR code on the tables.
